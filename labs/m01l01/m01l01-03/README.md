## Goal

You will know the difference between a terminal and a shell, and what Bash is.

In the lesson: Let us watch one turn. You type a command and press enter, and the word hello comes back. Now look at the second line: there are three spaces between the two words, and the answer has only one. Nothing trimmed your text. The shell split the line into words, the spaces between words are separators and vanish, and echo received two arguments which it printed with a single space between them. That is the evaluate step leaving fingerprints. The third line is what failure looks like: a word that names nothing, no program of that name anywhere, and the shell reports it by name rather than guessing at what you might have meant. The shell never guesses.
