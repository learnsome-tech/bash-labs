## Goal

Use parameter expansion to trim prefixes and suffixes, replace text, and change the case of strings.

In the lesson: We assign a word with mixed case. Two commas will turn every letter to lower case. Two carets will turn every letter to upper case. This is very useful for normalizing input before you check its value.
