## Goal

You will be able to create, copy, rename, and delete files and directories.

In the lesson: When you do mean it, this is the shape. A small tree we made ourselves, with two files inside it, listed so we can see what is about to go. Then the recursive removal, which takes the directory, the files under it and any directories under those, and says nothing whatsoever. The check confirms there is nothing left. Read a recursive removal out loud before you press enter, and read the argument rather than the option, because the argument is the part that decides how much disappears.
